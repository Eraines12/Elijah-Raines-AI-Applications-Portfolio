# Ethical Impacts of Generative AI (ChatGPT)

## Overview
This project analyzes the ethical and societal impacts of generative AI systems like ChatGPT, focusing on both the benefits and risks of these technologies.

## Key Areas
- Bias in AI systems  
- Misinformation risks  
- Privacy and data concerns  
- Job displacement and societal impact  

## What I Learned
- How generative AI systems work  
- The importance of responsible AI development  
- Ethical challenges in real-world AI use  

## Challenges
- Lack of transparency in AI models  
- Over-reliance on AI systems  
- Managing data privacy
