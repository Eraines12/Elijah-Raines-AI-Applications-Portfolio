# AGI in Healthcare

## Overview
This project explores how Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) could transform the healthcare industry. Unlike traditional AI, AGI has the potential to think, learn, and adapt across multiple domains, making it more powerful for complex medical decision-making.

## What I Did
- Analyzed current healthcare challenges such as:
  - Staff shortages
  - Rising costs
  - Limited access to care
- Studied how AI is currently used (diagnostics, prediction models)
- Proposed AGI-based solutions for:
  - Personalized medicine
  - Hospital optimization
  - Early disease detection

## Key Concepts Learned
- Difference between AI vs AGI
- Data-driven healthcare systems
- Predictive analytics in medicine
- Real-time decision systems

## Real-World Impact
AGI could:
- Improve diagnosis accuracy
- Reduce hospital wait times
- Deliver personalized treatment plans
- Expand healthcare access globally

## Challenges & Ethics
- Data privacy concerns
- Bias in medical data
- Accountability for AI decisions
