# AI in Government & Public Sector

## Overview
This project analyzes how artificial intelligence is used in government systems, especially during real-world events like the COVID-19 pandemic, and explores future applications such as smart city infrastructure.

## Key Areas
- Pandemic response and outbreak prediction  
- Data-driven decision making  
- Smart city AI systems  
- Public safety and resource management  

## What I Learned
- How AI processes large-scale public data  
- Predictive modeling in real-world scenarios  
- AI’s role in improving government efficiency  

## Challenges
- Privacy concerns and surveillance risks  
- High implementation costs  
- Public trust in AI systems
